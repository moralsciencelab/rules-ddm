# rule enforcement ddm

This repository contains the materials, data, and analysis scripts for a series of online studies in which people judged whether certain behavior are rule violations or not, analyzed using drift diffusion modelling.

Each folder contains all the files associated with one study:

* **violate**: People judged whether or not certain behaviors are rule violations under soft time pressure.
* **comply**: People judged whether or not certain behaviors are rule-compliant under soft time pressure.
* **congruency-proportions**: People judged whether or not certain behaviors are rule violations under soft time pressure. We varied the proportion of congruent vs. incongruent trials per block.
* **speed-accuracy**: People judged whether or not certain behaviors are rule violations. We varied whether they should prioritize speed vs. accuracy.
* **stroop-flanker**: People judged whether or not certain behaviors are rule violations under soft time pressure. In addition, they completed blocks of stroop and flanker tests.
* **punishment**: People judged whether or not certain behaviors should be punished under soft time pressure.
